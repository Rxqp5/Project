The Hive Falls

Pick a defender, then click open ground to deploy it. Click a deployed defender to upgrade, change its targeting, or sell it.

A Tyranid hive fleet is seeding the hive world with spores. Each spore you pop releases the weaker spore inside. Any that reach the gate drain your Faith.

Watch for: camouflaged spores (need Psykers or an auspex), Carnifex spores (need armour-piercing weapons), Zoanthrope spores (shrug off explosions), Pyrovore spores (shrug off fire).
