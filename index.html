Drag a unit from Requisition onto open ground to deploy it (or click the unit, then click the ground). Click a deployed unit to open its skill tree, change its targeting, or sell it.

Every unit has one shared upgrade, then two doctrines. Choosing a doctrine locks the other.

Armour vs AP: each point of armour above a weapon's AP removes one damage, down to a 20% minimum. Psychic attacks ignore armour.

Fliers take the short route across the sky. Whirlwinds and Dreadnought fists can't hit them.
